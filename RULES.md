# Real on Court — Game Rules

> Living document. Every rule the owner raises is recorded here and kept up to date.
> Last updated: 2026-05-20

## Concept
- Every padel court in the world has a reigning champion pair — the "Real on Court" (kings/queens of the court).
- A court's physical location is identified using **what3words (w3w)**.
- Padel is doubles: every pair is **2 players**.

## Holding the title
- The reigning champions keep the title until they are **beaten in a match** or they **forfeit**.

## Challenges
- An aspirant pair can **challenge** the champions of a court.
- A challenge **books the court for at least 90 minutes**. The aspirant chooses a **kick-off time**; the 90-minutes-or-more duration is assumed.
- Time categories:
  - **Morning:** 05:00–17:00
  - **Evening:** 17:00–05:00 (i.e. 5pm through to 5am)
  - **Weekend:** Saturday and Sunday (any time)

## Forfeit (champions lose the court without playing)
- Within **one natural week**, if the champions do **not accept** challenges that cover **all three categories** — a **morning**, an **evening**, AND a **weekend** — the court is **forfeited** and becomes **vacant**.
- This prevents an aspirant from forcing a forfeit by spamming challenges at the same inconvenient time.

## Matches and the title
- When the champions **accept** a challenge, the match is played and the **winners hold the title**:
  - Champions win → they keep the crown.
  - Challengers win → they become the new champions.
- When a court is **vacant**, aspirants play each other; the **winners become the new champions**.

## Pair-breaking (defending with a substitute)
- A defending pair can be broken: if **one** champion cannot play, the **other** champion may defend with a **different partner**. If they win, they **keep defending** the title, and the new champion lineup becomes *the champion who played + the substitute*.
- When a champion cannot play, their spot is **vacated** and their partner chooses the second player. The champion who cannot play has **three options**:
  1. **Accept** — agrees the spot is vacant; the available partner freely chooses the substitute.
  2. **Decline** — refuses the substitution (no substitute may be fielded).
  3. **Propose a player** — suggests a specific player; the available partner then makes the final choice of whether to take that proposed player.

## Approvals & confirmations
- **Player name approval:** when an aspirant enters another player's name (their own partner, or an opponent), that named player receives a **notification** and must **approve** being included. A challenge or match is not valid until every named player has approved.
- **Score confirmation:** a match result/score only counts once it is **confirmed by at least one player from each team**. The title changes hands (or a court is claimed/defended) only after both sides have confirmed the result.

## Player profiles & integrations
- Each player profile has a name and an optional **Playtomic level** (e.g. 3.5) and Playtomic profile link.
- Goal: display a player's **Playtomic ranking/level** inside Real on Court.
- **Playtomic sync:** Playtomic has no open public API for pulling rankings, so automatic sync would require an **official Playtomic partnership/API access**. Until then, players enter their Playtomic level manually (link to their Playtomic profile lets others verify it).
